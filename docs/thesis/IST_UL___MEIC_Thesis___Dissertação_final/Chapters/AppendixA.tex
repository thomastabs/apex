Finance, Banking, and Regulated Industries (Including Insurance) & Specialized LLMs can understand the "regulatory requirements, security needs, and performance constraints" especially in blockchain, cryptocurrency, smart contracts, and regulated environments. \cite{Haque:2025la, Kim:2025ao, Jensen:2025me } & 8 &  \cite{Basha:2025tt, Haque:2025la, Kim:2025ao, Jensen:2025me, AlHashimi:2025er, Zhang:2025ea, Banh:2025cf, Ramos:2025aa} \\ \addlinespace

Transportation and Safety-Critical Domains & The lack of transparency of LLMs is problematic, particularly in "safety-critical applications like healthcare, finance, or aerospace, where understanding the reasoning behind code is essential for ensuring security and correctness".\cite{Ahmed:2025ai} & 7 & \cite{Ahmed:2025ai, Haque:2025la, Basha:2025tt, Hemmat:2025rd, Wang:2025rs, Yoo:2025sa, Ramos:2025aa} \\ \addlinespace

Healthcare, Medical, and Biomedical Research & AI "accelerates the coding process by converting natural language or abstract research intent (a vibe) into functioning software modules, dramatically shortening development cycles in biomedical research and clinical application." \cite{Moore:2025vc}. & 5 & \cite{Moore:2025vc, Basha:2025tt, Hou:2025cl, Zhang:2025ea, Bai:2025ci} \\ \addlinespace

Education and Training & LLMs "directly support learning processes through interaction and conceptual clarification". Students should be taught "how to specify AI, including functional, non- functional, and 'happy and unhappy path scenarios'" and learn "prompt engineering workarounds" and to "research and fact- check AI responses". \cite{Ramos:2025aa} & 5 & \cite{Ramos:2025aa, Hemmat:2025rd, Zamfiroiu:2025ec, Zhang:2025ea, Hou:2025cl} \\ \addlinespace

Legal Reasoning and Regulatory Compliance & LLMs demonstrate success across multiple fields, such as education, healthcare, and legal reasoning. Specifically, prompt engineering is used to "tailor LLMs for the US legal system, enabling the model to identify relevant cases and predict judicial outcomes with greater precision". \cite{Jabrw:2025ss} & 4 &  \cite{Jabrw:2025ss, Bai:2025ci, Ramos:2025aa, AlHashimi:2025er} \\ \addlinespace

Digital Ecosystems (E-Commerce and Services) & Case studies involve the development of systems such as a food order and delivery system (FODS) and a smart wallet system (SWS). LLMs are applied in contexts like Online Retailers and the service industry. \cite{Kim:2025ao, Zhang:2025ea} & 3 & \cite{AlHashimi:2025er, Kim:2025ao, Zhang:2025ea} \\ \addlinespace

\end{longtable}
